Universities

Counting: each grant counts once, under its administering organisation and funding_commencement_year. A grant matches the FoR filter if any code in for_all_codes matches, so a single grant can appear under several fields. Universities are ranked by grant count for the current filters; the list shows the total for the selected years.
FoR classification change: ARC grants use ANZSRC FoR 2008 (divisions 01–22) up to 2022 and FoR 2020 (divisions 30–52) from 2023 (fellowships switch during 2022). The division filter maps each FoR 2008 division to its nearest FoR 2020 division so that trends run across the change (e.g. 08 → 46 Information and Computing Sciences). 10 Technology and 11 Medical and Health Sciences are split by 4-digit group (e.g. 1117 Public Health → 42 Health Sciences). This is an approximate crosswalk, not the official ABS field-level concordance, so a step at 2022/23 can be an artefact of reclassification. 45 Indigenous Studies has no FoR 2008 equivalent. 4- and 6-digit filters are exact codes and therefore cover only one classification era.
Share is the group’s grants as a percentage of all grants matching the current scheme and FoR filter in that year, which removes changes in overall scheme size (e.g. Discovery Projects fell from ~930 awards in 2011 to ~420–530 in 2023–26).
Organisations are shown as recorded by the ARC Data Portal. From 2024 some grants are recorded under “Adelaide University” (the merger of The University of Adelaide and the University of South Australia) while others still use the original names. Go8 = ANU, Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW, UQ, Monash, UWA and The University of Adelaide; “Adelaide University” is also counted as Go8, so a few 2024–26 grants that originated at UniSA fall into Go8. State is the administering organisation’s state or territory (head office for multi-state bodies such as Australian Catholic University and CSIRO). Linkage Projects are not included (no FoR codes in the current crawl).
